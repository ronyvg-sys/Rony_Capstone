# Project ABC — Project Charter

## Project Information
Project: ABC — Banking Single Billing Transformation
Client: Global Bank
Project Sponsor: Head of Corporate Banking Technology
Project Manager: Rony George
Delivery Model: Agile
Project Start: January 2026
Target Production Release: December 18, 2026

## Objective
Project ABC will consolidate multiple independent billing platforms into a single enterprise billing solution for corporate banking services.

## Business Objectives
- Simplify billing operations.
- Reduce duplicate billing platforms.
- Improve billing transparency.
- Provide consistent customer billing.
- Reduce operational support effort.
- Establish a scalable billing platform for future products.

## Scope
The project includes billing migration, customer billing validation, integration testing, user acceptance testing, production readiness, and migration of in-scope billing products.

## Out of Scope
Changes to unrelated retail banking billing platforms and non-billing customer applications are outside the project scope.

## Success Criteria
- Successful migration of all in-scope billing products.
- UAT completion with no open Severity 1 defects.
- Production readiness approval.
- Successful reconciliation between legacy and target billing.
